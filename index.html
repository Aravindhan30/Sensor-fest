About SENSORA

Showcase. Learn.
Innovate Together.

SENSORA 2K26 is an exclusive ECE department event — a sensor seminar and live exhibition designed for our own students. Our motive is to showcase the maximum number of sensors possible, spark deep curiosity, and give every ECE student hands-on understanding of how real-world sensors and embedded systems work.

"Real Sensors. Real Skills. Our Department. Our Pride."
